# Odysseus – Tiny Orbit Privacy Policy

This policy covers the Odysseus bot operated by the Tiny Orbit team in the Tiny Orbit Discord server.

## What the bot accesses

When a team member asks Odysseus to review feedback, the bot retrieves up to 20 recent messages from the server’s `📥│feedback-inbox` channel. It passes excerpts of player messages, author display names or usernames, timestamps, and links to the original messages into the team’s Odysseus session. It uses text from the feedback channel and skips messages with no readable text and messages from other bots.

The bot also uses server and channel identifiers to route approved announcements and patch notes to their designated channels. It returns a link to each message it posts.

## How the team uses the information

The Tiny Orbit team uses this information to review playtest reports, summarize recurring feedback, and investigate issues in the game. Feedback summaries are generated using a locally hosted Ollama model. The team does not use Discord messages to train AI models. When the bot publishes an approved update, the update text is sent to Discord for delivery.

## Storage and retention

The bot itself does not maintain a separate archive of feedback messages. Excerpts and summaries can remain in the team’s local Odysseus chat history or in saved playtest notes. The team keeps those copies only while they are needed to review the feedback or address the reported issue, then removes them. The original messages remain in Discord and are managed separately by Discord and the server.

## How to ask for access, correction, or deletion

Contact a Tiny Orbit moderator by direct message in Discord and say that your request is about Odysseus. Include the original message link if you have it, or the approximate date and your Discord username so the team can find the relevant feedback. The team will handle requests to access, correct, or delete the feedback it holds, including copies in local Odysseus chats and saved notes. You can delete your original Discord message separately. Deleting it in Discord does not automatically remove saved copies from Odysseus.
